## 👩‍💻 Open Source

Many of our in-house tools and libraries are available to the public under permissive licenses.  
We use these tools every day to develop our apps, and we hope they can be of use to you too!

### FoundationPlus
> Foundation+ is a large collection of extensions to Swift's `Foundation` types.  
> It adds over a hundred functions, operator overloads and convenience initializers.
>
> [GitHub](https://github.com/FiveSheepCo/FoundationPlus)

### SwiftUIElements
> SwiftUIElements extends `SwiftUI` with useful views and extensions.  
> It adds compatibility modifiers, better binding ergonomics, easy programmatic navigation and a lot more.
>
> [GitHub](https://github.com/FiveSheepCo/SwiftUIElements)

### SwiftDataSync
> SwiftDataSync is the missing programmable sync layer for `SwiftData`.  
> It enables native, granular and ergonomic iCloud sync for your data models.
>
> [GitHub](https://github.com/FiveSheepCo/SwiftDataSync)

### PurchaseKit
> PurchaseKit makes setting up IAPs and subscriptions extremely easy.  
> It provides correct, observable and trustworthy purchase and entitlement handling.
>
> [GitHub](https://github.com/FiveSheepCo/PurchaseKit)

### MapItemPicker
> MapItemPicker provides a high-quality location picking UI.  
> It abstracts away a lot of MapKit complexity and quirks, and "just works".
>
> [GitHub](https://github.com/FiveSheepCo/MapItemPicker)
